# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in this project's issues, pull requests, discussions and
other project spaces should be treated with respect, regardless of background,
experience, disability, identity or personal characteristics.

Be constructive and specific when reviewing work. Critique the change, explain
your reasoning and make room for newcomers. Respect others' time and privacy.
Disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual
harassment, impersonation, sustained disruption and publication of another
person's private information are not.

## Reporting and enforcement

The project maintainer is [@benczb](https://github.com/benczb). For non-sensitive
moderation requests, contact the maintainer in the affected project thread. For
sensitive reports, use a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub
profile. If none is available, open a neutral issue asking for a private reporting
channel; do not identify affected people or describe the incident publicly.
Immediate platform abuse can also be reported through GitHub's reporting tools.

The maintainer will review reports, seek relevant context and choose an appropriate
response. Responses may include a request to change behavior, removal of content,
a warning, or temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Enforcement
applies consistently, including to regular contributors. Retaliation against a
person who reports a concern is unacceptable. Share reports only as needed to
investigate and respond; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
